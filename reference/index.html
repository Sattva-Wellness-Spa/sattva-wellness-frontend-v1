The idea behind Sattva

Five elements, held in balance

Ayurveda holds that the body and the universe are made of the same five elements, the Pancha Mahabhuta. When they fall out of balance we feel it as stiffness, fatigue or restlessness. Every therapy at Sattva is chosen to bring one of them back into line.

पृथ्वी

Prithvi · Earth

Grounding, structure, steadiness in the muscles and joints.

जल

Jal · Water

Fluidity and circulation, carried by warm oil.

अग्नि

Agni · Fire

Warmth that softens deep tension. Deep work for knotted muscles.

वायु

Vayu · Air

Breath and movement, eased by slow rhythmic strokes.

आकाश

Akash · Space

The quiet you leave with. Room to think again.

Good to know

Questions guests ask

Which branch should I visit?

Whichever is closer. Sector 83 is in Vlante D Block Market, New Gurgaon. Sector 64 is in R R Market, opposite M3M Urbana Premium. Both offer the full Sattva menu at the same prices.

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome when a room is free. Evenings and weekends fill quickly, and couple massages and the jacuzzi are best booked ahead. A WhatsApp booking guarantees your slot.

How do the packages work?

You pay once and use your sessions over the validity period: one year for Essential Wellness and Premium Indulgence, lifetime for Friends & Family, which up to 4–5 people in your group can share. Packages are non-refundable.

Can I use my package at both branches?

Yes. A package bought at Sector 83 or Sector 64 can be redeemed at either branch. Mix and match visits however suits you.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. We provide disposable undergarments, a robe and towels, and keep you draped throughout the massage.

How do you keep things hygienic?

Fresh linen and towels for every guest, sanitised surfaces between sessions, and sealed single-use items wherever possible.

Can I buy a session as a gift?

Yes. Ask at reception or message us for a gift voucher for any therapy and duration.
